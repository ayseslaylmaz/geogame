WELCOME

AYSSKY CARGO

A geography and logistics game.

1. Load cargo

Travel to active cargo hubs and load packages. Level 1 starts from Ankara.

2. Plan your route

Choose your destination, manage fuel, coins and time carefully.

3. Deliver correctly

When you arrive, answer the geography question correctly to complete the delivery.

4. Earn and upgrade

Gain coins from deliveries, upgrade your aircraft and unlock the next levels.

  • Score comes only from completed orders.
  • Wrong answer = 5 seconds penalty.
  • Hint = 3 coins.
  • Level 2 adds İstanbul as a cargo hub.
  • Level 3 adds Dubai as a cargo hub.

GAME GUIDE

How to Play ✈

Become a cargo pilot and complete deliveries by exploring the world!

01
Collect your cargo

Go to a cargo hub and press Load Packages. Each order must fit completely in your plane.

02
Select an airport

Click an airport on the map. Check the distance, flight time, fuel and coin cost.

03
Fly and deliver

Press Start Flight. At the destination, answer the geography question correctly to complete your delivery.

04
Earn coins and upgrade

Deliver packages to earn coins. Buy fuel when needed and upgrade your aircraft at Ankara.

⭐ Important Rules

✓ Completed order: +20 points

✓ Completed level: +25 bonus points

✕ Wrong answer: −5 seconds

💡 Hint: 3 coins

⛽ Flights require enough fuel, coins and time

🌍 Game Levels

Level 1 · Türkiye 4 orders · 120 sec · Ankara hub
Level 2 · Europe 5 orders · 210 sec · Ankara + İstanbul
Level 3 · World 4 orders · 360 sec · Ankara + İstanbul + Dubai
